1. The service

CamHunt is a tool for reviewing possible hidden-camera clues using the capabilities of your Android device. Its results help guide your own inspection. A reflection, infrared response, magnetic disturbance, recognised object, network response or Bluetooth advertisement does not establish that a hidden camera is present, and a scan that finds nothing does not establish that an area is free of cameras. Missing hardware, permissions, interrupted checks, camera sensitivity and device or network conditions limit results, and the app tells you when a scan's coverage is limited or incomplete. When a decision affects your safety or legal rights, use additional inspection and appropriate advice.

The free version includes Lens Reflection, Magnetometer, Bluetooth Scanner and sharing a report as a screenshot, and may display ads.
